Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for common questions about your dashboard.

How do I connect IndiaMART?
Open the Auto-Scrape page and click "Connect IndiaMART". We'll open a one-time connect link in a new tab — follow the steps there while logged into seller.indiamart.com, and this dashboard will pick up the connection automatically once it's done.
Why is a lead locked?
Scraped IndiaMART buy-leads start locked until they're "unlocked" — unlocking spends IndiaMART credits from your account's credit balance. Each scrape or auto-scrape run unlocks new leads up to the limit you set, so buyer contact details only appear for leads you've chosen to spend credits on.
How do I add machines in bulk?
Use the CSV Upload page to drag and drop a CSV of machine names — they'll be added in one batch, with duplicates skipped automatically.
How do I cancel Pro?
Pro runs as a 30-day subscription from your last payment rather than an auto-renewing charge you need to cancel — it simply reverts to Free once it expires unless you upgrade again from the Payments page. If you have a billing question, reach out using the contact details below.
What does Auto-Scrape actually do?
Once IndiaMART is connected, Auto-Scrape runs on the interval you choose (5–120 minutes) and unlocks up to your chosen limit (0–10) of new buy-leads each run, so you don't have to trigger scrapes manually.
